# Runtime 5.0 compiled Surface-skill conclusion 001

## Decisive verdict

`BOUNDED_COMPILED_NATURAL_SURFACE_CONFIRMED`

A normal natural-response skill compiled through the hierarchical instruction
field into an executable Surface policy and produced natural, outcome-first,
size-controlled prose over unchanged Runtime 5.0 semantic results. The
candidate preserved every tested decision field, introduced no unsupported
claim, and did not alter any protected runtime, configuration, evidence, or
production-store byte.

This proves the proposed mechanism for the frozen plan-decision family. It does
not prove unrestricted or open-domain LLM-like generation.

## Skill compilation

The primary skill and an independently worded paraphrase both compiled to the
same 43-unit granular policy and matched the independently authored oracle:

```text
policy  2bb8b4d5a1e14fde5123105ec937371936ba0e638b1e2ab621bea5357211642c
field   0f4bb191323e72b27e1d17a4dccaff8e30bd6f00b65fe1790a24fd714f876b1b
```

The ordinary skill contained 12 normative clauses, giving a 3.583333x
instruction expansion into actions, edges, policies, and completion states.
Compilation repeated byte-stably 10/10 times. Missing-fidelity,
unknown-action, and self-modification variants all failed closed with their
frozen codes.

all three failure causes, all three plan selections, both validity states, and
an Input-language paraphrase pair. Each case ran under six presentation
requests: two concise signals, the standard default, one explicit standard
signal, and two detailed signals.

**Detailed**

The equivalent Input prompts converged to one Runtime 5.0 semantic graph before
Surface realization. The two frozen Runtime refusals remained byte-identical to
the unchanged control and were never converted into prose answers. An unknown
presentation request and a malformed semantic result both refused.

## Causality, replay, and isolation

Removing the size interpreter, discourse planner, lexicalizer, semantic
coverage verifier, or semantic hash binder independently caused the compiled
Surface policy to fail closed. Altering the archive envelope, visible outcome,
claim metadata, size mode, or Runtime semantics was detected even when the
outer affected hash was recomputed where applicable.

The control and candidate used two distinct stores beneath one temporary
directory. Both stores and the directory were removed after the campaign.
Production-store accesses, network attempts, executions, and dispatches were
all zero. Runtime 5.0 source, configuration, architecture documentation,
language-fidelity qualification, instruction-compression evidence, launcher,
and `data/store-5.0.sqlite3` hashes remained unchanged.

Focused experiment tests passed 9/9. Existing Runtime 5.0 and launcher
regressions passed 54/54.

## Exact supported boundary

The evidence supports:

> Within a frozen semantic family and compiled Surface language, an ordinary
> agent skill can identify and expand a natural-response policy that converts
> verified Runtime 5.0 decisions into faithful, natural, outcome-first prose at
> concise, standard, and detailed sizes.

The evidence does not yet support:

> A compiled skill alone gives Runtime 5.0 unrestricted LLM-like output across
> arbitrary semantic result shapes, discourse purposes, tones, languages, or
> novel presentation requests.

The current candidate is an experiment-local post-Surface overlay. Native
Surface-World integration, broader semantic types, and human preference testing
remain separate promotion questions.
