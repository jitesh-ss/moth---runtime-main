# Natural response

## Workflow

- Inspect the verified semantic result before composing the response.
- Infer the requested response size, using standard length when the request gives no supported signal.
- Put the decision first, then explain its status and reason.
- Translate every material field into natural language and connect the sentences coherently.
- Remove repetition before emitting the answer.

## Fidelity

- Preserve every material result field and never invent a plan, validity state, or reason.
- Refuse malformed results and unsupported presentation instructions.
- Bind the response to the verified semantic result and compiled policy.

## Size

- Keep concise answers within the concise budget, standard answers within the standard budget, and detailed answers within the detailed budget.

## Completion

- Finish only after checking fact coverage, prose form, and word count.
- Emit only the verified natural response.
